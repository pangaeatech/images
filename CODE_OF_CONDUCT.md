# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for
everyone who participates in this project, regardless of personal background
or identity.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- be considerate, constructive, and professional;
- give specific feedback about work rather than attacking people;
- respect differing viewpoints and levels of experience;
- accept reasonable corrective feedback;
- protect confidential information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insulting or demeaning language,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, sharing another person's
private information, and sexualized or violent attention are not acceptable.
Conduct that would be unsafe or inappropriate in a professional environment
is also unacceptable here.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces, including when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to a repository maintainer listed in
[CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) through a private channel. Include any
relevant context while avoiding unnecessary private information.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, keep details limited to
those who need them, and take proportionate action. Possible actions include a
warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from
the project. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
